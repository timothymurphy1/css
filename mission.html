Our Mission

Community Savings Solution exists to restore dignity to saving. We are building a new architecture of community capital — where every saver’s small act of contribution becomes part of a permanent, empowering pool.

From the earliest analysis of the savings sector — from Ron Sandler’s review (2001) to Archbishop Justin Welby’s call (2017) for financial inclusion — the need has been clear: a structure that reaches those excluded and transforms the habit of saving into a shared act of restoration.

CSS, together with CTINS and Bridge Across Community, turns that intent into execution. The first milestone is simple but profound: light Saver One and begin Community One.
