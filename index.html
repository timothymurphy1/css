Coming Together on a Mission

There are a variety of vocal audiences who have been talking about the need for a Solution for the Savings Sector and to reach deep into communities, and to provide a mechanism to reach the excluded and those contending with the effects of Poverty, from Ron Sandler in 2001 to Justin Welby’s setup of the Just Finance Foundation in 2017.

Action in a 1st Community begins with getting User 1.

Never is it a case that something can begin and suddenly the system is everywhere. There is a subtlety in inception. This is a new system and has to be taught. Getting User 1 is the building block for all of the Milestones in Stage 1 and the stages that follow.
